# HMPL Whitepaper v1.1 (post-JOLT tone)

HMPL is an institutional governance standard for AI systems used in high-stakes or regulated domains. It treats the internalization of meaning as an architectural violation and replaces behavioral oversight with design-time incapacity: the Artificial Domain cannot host meaning or author law.

HMPL enforces dual-domain governance (Human H vs Artificial A) joined only by a constrained interface (“Dash”) that transfers structured state and blocks narrative. When ambiguity, policy collision, or irreversibility arises, escalation is mandatory. Human decisions are compiled into signed, versioned policy bundles; policy evolution is controlled by authority classes and recorded as cryptographic audit events. Trust is based on proof, not explainability claims.
