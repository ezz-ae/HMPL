
# HMPL (arXiv Submission Wrapper)

Abstract:
HMPL is a proposed constitutional governance framework that enforces design-time incapacity: artificial execution systems cannot internalize meaning or author law. HMPL separates Human judgment from Artificial execution, mandates escalation under ambiguity and irreversibility, and derives trust from cryptographic audit events.
