# HMPL vs Dubai’s Human–Machine Collaboration Icons (HMC)

Dubai’s **Human–Machine Collaboration (HMC) Icons** are a **disclosure taxonomy**: they label *how much* human vs machine contribution was involved in producing research or content, and *where* in the workflow the machine contributed (e.g., ideation, analysis, writing). They are designed for transparency and credibility in publications.

**HMPL** is different: it is a **governance architecture and enforcement protocol** for high‑stakes systems where machines can cause real‑world impact. HMPL defines two disjoint domains (Human judgment vs Machine execution), constrains what may cross the interface (“the Dash”), and mandates escalation/freeze, precedent-to-policy compilation, and auditable policy versioning.

Use them together: **HMC** to *disclose collaboration*, **HMPL** to *constrain authority and execution*.
