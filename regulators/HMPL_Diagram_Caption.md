**Figure X — HMPL as a controlled lab‑to‑factory pipeline.** Human authorities operate in a separate judgment domain that produces signed policy bundles and exception precedents. The AI domain is an execution engine that consumes only structured state, constraints, risk flags, calibrated confidence, and signed rules through “the Dash.” When ambiguity, policy collision, or irreversible impact is detected, the AI domain cannot improvise: it freezes and escalates. Governance actions (policy publish, freeze/rollback, certifications) are recorded on the H‑Ledger as hashes and signatures—enabling independent verification of obedience, containment, and human sovereignty without exposing user data or model internals.
